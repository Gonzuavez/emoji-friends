# Optional Bruno recordings

Place approved local dialogue files here and map their URLs to `audio.recognition`, `audio.introduction`, `audio.message`, and `audio.exit` in the gift configuration. Captions remain the source of the visible message. Use recordings matching the configured names and message. Keep recordings within each beat's duration (see `timeline.ts`), or adjust that beat before shipping. There is no synthesized speech or paid API.

The initial tap unlocks Web Audio. Optional recordings are decoded through that context. Missing/blocked audio does not interrupt the timeline; soft knocks are generated locally. Do not add final files until Angel/ChatGPT supplies approved voice and artwork.
